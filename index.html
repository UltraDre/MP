      Search movies to stream

      Search several catalogues of openly licensed video at once — Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons and the federated PeerTube network — for movies, series episodes and shorts offered as a direct video file or stream. Only records that declare a public-domain or Creative Commons license are listed; the claims come from uploaders and are not independently verified, so open the record to check the rights. This does not search paid streaming services or the whole web. Your query is sent to the selected catalogues.

      Enter a title to search public-domain and Creative Commons video files.
